# Product Blueprint

## Platform thesis
An intelligent workplace mobility platform that matches people, not just routes.

## Tenants
Each organization is an isolated tenant with its own branding, identity provider, locations, parking configuration, matching policy, privacy controls, incentives, and analytics.

## Member experience
- Organization sign-in / invite
- Profile and commute preferences
- Home-area privacy: store/use coarse geospatial origin for discovery; reveal precise pickup only after consent
- Commute intent: Quiet, Social, Networking, Fastest, Maximum Impact
- Offer or request a ride
- Ranked matches with explainable factors
- Mutual acceptance before ride formation
- In-app ride coordination
- Private post-ride feedback: Ride Again / Fine / Prefer Different Match
- Recurring commute circles
- Safety tools and trusted-contact sharing hooks

## Admin experience
- Tenant onboarding and branding
- SSO / SCIM configuration hooks
- Locations, campuses, shifts, and parking inventory
- Matching policy editor with guardrails
- Eligibility and privacy policies
- Incentive rules
- Ride/parking analytics
- Sustainability metrics
- Sentiment and repeat-match metrics
- Audit log

## Matching dimensions
- Route compatibility
- Schedule compatibility
- Ride preferences
- Commute intent
- Social compatibility
- Previous ride signals
- Reliability
- Parking impact
- Tenant policy constraints

## Non-goals for first release
- Public stranger marketplace
- Fare marketplace / driver profit model
- Autonomous payroll deductions
- Full background-check marketplace
